I am a junior undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at ShanghaiTech University. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jingyi Yu and Prof. Jiayuan Gu. I am also working with Prof. Shenlong Wang at UIUC as a summer research intern.
